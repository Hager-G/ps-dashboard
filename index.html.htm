📋 Slack Setup Guide
1
Go to api.slack.com/apps and sign in
2
Click "Create New App" → "From scratch"
3
Name your app "PS Dashboard" and select your workspace
4
In the left sidebar click "Incoming Webhooks" and toggle it ON
5
Scroll down, click "Add New Webhook to Workspace"
6
Choose the channel you want reports posted to (e.g. #problem-solve) → click Allow
7
Copy the webhook URL and paste it in the field above → Save Settings
8
Click "Test Connection" to verify, then use "Send Report" on the dashboard!
Note: When sending directly from a browser, Slack blocks the response due to CORS — but the message is delivered. You'll still get a confirmation. For a full backend proxy, see the README.
⚠️ Data Management
All data is saved automatically in your browser's local storage. It persists across sessions and browser restarts.
